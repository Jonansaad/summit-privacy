Endless SUMMIT — Support

Help for Endless SUMMIT, the mountain climbing game by Jaco.

Something not working, or a question about a purchase? Email us and we'll get back to you:

support@jacostudio.games

It helps if you tell us your device (for example iPhone 15), its iOS version, and what happened just before the problem.

Purchases

I bought coins but didn't get them

Close SUMMIT completely and open it again. The game checks with the App Store for any purchase still to be delivered each time it starts, and adds the coins then. If they still don't appear, email us with the date of the purchase. You can find the receipt in your App Store purchase history.

Remove ads is gone on my new phone

Open the coin store (the Coins button at camp) and tap Restore purchases. Remove ads is tied to your Apple ID, so it comes back on any device signed in to the same account.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple. You can ask Apple for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do my coins and camp carry over to a new phone?

Yes, on iPhone and iPad. SUMMIT keeps your save in your own iCloud account, so coins, camp, gear, relics and skins come back on any iPhone or iPad signed in to the same Apple Account. Make sure iCloud is on in Settings. Remove ads can also be restored with Restore purchases.

Playing

My progress is gone

If you're signed in to iCloud, reinstalling Endless SUMMIT brings your progress back: open the game, wait a few seconds at camp, and your coins and camp return. If they don't, check that iCloud is on (Settings > [your name] > iCloud) and that you're signed in with the same Apple Account as before, then close and reopen the game. Without iCloud, the save lives only on the phone and uninstalling deletes it. Settings > Reset expedition starts your climb again but keeps your coins.

The game feels slow or stutters

Close other apps, make sure Low Power Mode is off, and restart your phone. If it continues, email us your device model.

No sound

Check the sound button in the game's Settings, the ring/silent switch on your phone, and the volume.

Ads and privacy

How do I see fewer ads?

The optional Remove ads purchase stops the ad breaks between climbs. Watching an ad to continue a climb or double your coins is always your choice.

How do I change my ad choice?

Go to Settings > Privacy & ads in the game. You can switch between personalised and non-personalised ads at any time. On iPhone and iPad you can also change tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
