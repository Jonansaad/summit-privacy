SUMMIT — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Who we are

SUMMIT is a free mobile climbing game made by Jaco ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. It applies to SUMMIT on iPhone, iPad and Android.

The short version: we have no accounts and no servers that collect your data. Your progress stays on your phone. The game is paid for by ads, and our ad partners use some device data to show and measure those ads. You choose whether they may use it to personalise ads, and you can change that at any time in Settings > Privacy & ads.

We never see or store your name, email address, contacts, photos or payment details. Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google.

We do not sell your personal information. When you choose personalised ads, sharing your advertising ID with ad partners may count as "sharing" or "selling" under some US state laws; choosing non-personalised ads in the game opts you out of it.

Your choices

  • In Europe (EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland): before any ad appears, Google's consent message asks whether our ad partners may use your data. Nothing is shared before you answer, and you can change your answer any time in Settings > Privacy & ads.
  • Everywhere else: the first time ads could appear, SUMMIT asks whether you want personalised or non-personalised ads. Nothing is shared with ad partners before you answer. Change your answer any time in Settings > Privacy & ads. The game plays the same either way.
  • On iPhone and iPad: if you choose personalised ads, Apple asks whether SUMMIT may track you. If you say no, ads stay non-personalised. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • On Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Google > Ads (the exact path varies by phone).
  • Fewer ads: the optional Remove ads purchase stops the ad breaks between climbs. Watching an ad to continue a climb or to double your coins is always your choice.
  • Delete your game data: uninstalling SUMMIT deletes everything it saved on your device. Settings > Reset expedition clears your progress but keeps your coins.

Children

SUMMIT is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly allow personalised ads for children. If you believe a child's information has been shared with our ad partners, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or US states such as California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or limit its use, and to withdraw consent. Because we keep no personal information ourselves, the quickest way to use these rights is:

  • choose non-personalised ads in Settings > Privacy & ads, or uninstall the game;
  • contact Unity through its privacy policy for data its ad services hold;
  • or write to us and we will help.

You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

How long information is kept

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall SUMMIT or reset it. Our partners keep ad and purchase data for the periods set out in their policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
